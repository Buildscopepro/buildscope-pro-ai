## Measurement / takeoff
Wall quantities use length × height minus openings, then waste.
Ceiling quantities use length × width, then waste.
Sheet/paint quantities use coverage values and round up to purchase units.

## Product-backed design rule
Every client-facing wall or ceiling finish can be linked to:
- real product name
- brand
- model/SKU for exact claims
- color and finish
- size
- quantity/unit
- retailer / where to buy
- price/availability snapshot
- Exact / Equivalent / Pending status

A pending finish is not ready for final client approval.
